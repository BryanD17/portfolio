## The problem

An App Store listing is not a pitch. It gives you a name, a few screenshots,
and no room to explain why an app that watches your form is different from the
forty other fitness trackers on the same page. StayFit needed somewhere to
make that case properly.

## What I built

A dark, product-led marketing site built with Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS,
and Motion, deployed on Vercel. The home page pairs the calendar-first pitch
("Show up. Watch your year fill in.") with live app mockups and funnels
visitors to the App Store listing and a free-trial flow, backed by feature,
coaches, pricing, and FAQ sections. The nutrition section goes beyond static
marketing with an interactive demo: type a meal and see the breakdown, with
the caveat stated right on the page that estimates are always confirmable and
never automatic.
