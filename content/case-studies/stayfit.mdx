## The problem

Fitness apps ask you to trust that you're doing the movement correctly. They
count reps; they don't watch your form. And almost all of them fall over the
moment you walk into a gym basement with no signal, which is exactly where
people use them.

## What I built

A native iOS application spanning workout and nutrition tracking, a social
feed, a creator marketplace with real payouts, an Apple Watch companion, and
home-screen widgets. 80,000+ lines of Swift across 260+ files, organized as 58
services behind an MVVM + Combine architecture.

## The hard parts

### Real-time form check, on device

I built an exercise form-check engine on Apple's Vision framework that scores
live body-pose and joint angles on-device to give instant coaching feedback.
Running it on-device rather than in the cloud meant feedback arrives fast
enough to correct a rep while it's happening, and no video ever leaves the
phone.

### Offline-first sync

Gyms have terrible signal, so the app assumes the network is gone. I built a
persisted mutation queue with last-write-wins conflict resolution, so a full
workout logged in a basement reconciles cleanly the moment connectivity
returns.

### Payments, AI, and rewards on the edge

12 Supabase/PostgreSQL edge functions power Stripe Connect payouts to
creators, AI chat, and referral rewards, keeping payment logic and API keys
off the client entirely.
